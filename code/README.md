# Checks of the paper

Every computation of the paper is listed below with its script and its complete recorded output. `code/rerun.sh NAME ...` reruns a script by name and compares its output with the recorded one (timings and PARI's stack warnings removed), or, for the scripts without a recorded output, relies on the exit status of a script that compares the results itself. `code/rerun.sh --list` prints the names; `SAVE=dir code/rerun.sh ...` also keeps the outputs.

Layout: `impl1/` is the program of the computation: the plantri output filter, the guide of plantri 5.8 (`plantri-guide.txt`, unchanged) and the enumeration scripts in `impl1/enum/`, the Rust programs of the first level, the search and the replay in `impl1/rust/`, the replay and coverage scripts in `impl1/`, the recorded outputs in `impl1/out/`; `gp/` and `sage/` hold the PARI/GP and SageMath scripts with their outputs (`.out` next to each script); `hyps/` computes the range of d of the program (Computation B.4); `lean-data/` holds the generators of the data of the Lean proofs, and `lean/numerics/` those of the enclosures of Proposition 4.6 and of the margins printed in Section 4.3; `lean/` holds the checks of the Lean package; `vendor/` the scripts that copied the eight-point development (`THIRD_PARTY.md`); `paper/` the figures and the statement map.

Times are wall clock on an AMD Ryzen 9 5900X (24 threads), with `J` parallel jobs where stated, while other work was running. They were measured on the recorded runs and are indications only. Some records keep the times of their run: the columns `wall_s` and `user_s` of `impl1/out/guarded/replay_summary.txt` and the `.time` and `.log` files of `impl1/out/guarded/jobs/` (the replay), `lean/build.out`, `lean/check.out` and `lean/comparator.out` (the Lean build, check and Comparator run), `lean-data/regen.out`, `vendor/em8_names.out` and `vendor/generalize.out`. `code/rerun.sh` leaves times out of its comparisons and prints the time of each script it reruns. The continuous integration runs every script marked CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`, 4-core runners). The replay reads the unpacked release assets (`ASSETS.md`, `impl1/fetch_assets.sh DIR`); the jobs `replay` and `replay-join` of `ci.yml` run it on the tarballs of the release, or of its draft before the release is published.

The recorded PARI/GP outputs were made with PARI/GP 2.17.4; the gp job of CI installs PARI/GP 2.15.4 (Ubuntu 24.04) and compares its outputs with the recorded ones.

## Continuous integration

The workflow `ci.yml` runs on GitHub's runners at each push that touches the Lean package, the code or the data, and when started by hand. Its first job reads the files of the pushed commits: the Lean jobs run when they touch a Lean source, the Lake files, `config.json` or `code/lean/`, the computation jobs when they touch `code/` outside `code/lean/`, `data/` or `ASSETS.md`; a change of `ci.yml`, a new branch, a pull request and a run by hand run every job. The Lean jobs run `code/lean/check.sh` (the build of the package, compiling only what changed since the last build, restored from the cache or from the latest release; every target up to date; a scan of the sources for the constructs that can bypass the kernel; `#print axioms` of the theorems of `code/lean/axioms.lean`, `Tammes15.conjecture_of_enum_killed` among them; the types of the interfaces; an audit of every constant of the package), a scan for `sorry`, `admit` and `native_decide`, and Comparator on the theorems of `config.json`, in nanoda alone (`lean_kernel` false in `config.json`, a patch of this repository on Comparator); a run of Comparator whose exporter crashes during the export, before any kernel runs, is repeated once, and the repetition decides. The computation jobs rerun the computations marked CI below and compare each output with the recorded one: the enumeration and the first level on every part, `tie_targets`, the PARI/GP scripts marked CI and the range of d of `code/hyps/`, and the replay of every certificate of the second level in three parallel jobs, followed by the totals and the coverage join on these replays (the longest replay jobs take about 53 min each by their recorded times); the replay jobs read the data tarballs of `ASSETS.md` from a release or its draft, and a run started by hand fails when none holds them. On a tag, `release.yml` takes the `ci.yml` run started by hand on the tagged commit, checks through the GitHub API that every job of that run except the badges passed, and attaches the PDF, the outputs of its checks and the build that Comparator checked to the release; it compiles nothing but the PDF, and takes the data tarballs of the previous release when the sha256 lines of `ASSETS.md` have not changed. A run started by hand on `main` writes the three badges of `README.md` from its jobs: the hypotheses and axioms of the theorems of `config.json` (`code/lean/facts.lean`), the kernels of Comparator, and the computation jobs that passed; each badge links to that run.

- The search itself on all 462,703 graphs (about 11 core hours); CI replays every tree it wrote.

## The record of the coverage join

The first line of `impl1/out/guarded/coverage_join.txt` names the directory of the job records it joined, `code/impl1/out/guarded/jobs`; a join of the same records in another directory differs from it in that line only.
