The largest minimal angular distance of fifteen points on the unit sphere is arccos u ≈ 53.6578501°, where u is the root in (1/2, 7/10) of `13u^5 - u^4 + 6u^3 + 2u^2 - 3u - 1`; it is attained by two configurations described by Buddenhagen and Kottwitz, whose contact graphs are not isomorphic.

This is AI-generated research: the results, proofs and code were found and written by AI. Credit goes to all the humans whose work it builds on.

**Proved in Lean 4:** `Tammes15.reduction`, with Mathlib and only the standard axioms, proves `Tammes15.Conjecture` from five hypotheses: `KappaHyp` (D1, the rigidity bound), `EnumComplete` (D2, the completeness of the list of plane graphs), `Killed` (D3, the refutation of every graph), `AttainedHyp` (D4, the attainment) and `FejesTothBound` (the bound of Fejes Tóth, from the literature, not proved in Lean). Comparator checks it against `Tammes15/Challenge.lean` with the Lean kernel and nanoda. **By computation:** D1 to D4, by programs that are not verified in Lean: SageMath and PARI/GP for D1 and D4, plantri for D2, a linear filter and an interval search whose certificates are replayed for D3 (D3 as stated is checked by this program only); a second program written separately, with relations outside the relation system of D3, refutes every graph again; it is a second computation of the theorem, not a check of D3. `code/rerun.sh NAME` reruns a check and compares its output with the recorded one; `code/README.md` lists every check with its script and recorded output.
