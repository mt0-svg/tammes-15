## The formal statements

```lean
theorem Tammes15.nonunique_of_enum_progTreesDom (L : Set Tammes15.PlaneGraph) (N : Tammes15.PaperSteps.Procs)
    (h2 : Tammes15.EnumComplete L) (h3 : Tammes15.Contractors.ProgTreesDom L N) (hN : Tammes15.Contractors.Impl N) :
    ∃ d : ℝ, IsGreatest {d | Tammes15.Achievable 15 d} d ∧

        IsEmpty
          (Tammes15.Nonunique.contactGraph X (Real.cos d) ≃g Tammes15.Nonunique.contactGraph Y (Real.cos d))

theorem Tammes15.conjecture_of_enum_killed (L : Set Tammes15.PlaneGraph) (h2 : Tammes15.EnumComplete L)
    (h3 : Tammes15.Killed L {Tammes15.Attained.frameC1, Tammes15.Attained.frameC3}) :
    Tammes15.Conjecture
```

`Tammes15.Conjecture` (`Tammes15/Statement.lean`, Mathlib definitions only) says that $`\arccos u`$ is the greatest $`d`$ such that some fifteen points of the unit sphere are pairwise at angular distance at least $`d`$, for the root $`u`$ of $`13u^5-u^4+6u^3+2u^2-3u-1`$ in $`(1/2, 7/10)`$. The second theorem is Corollary 1.2 in the same form.

The first theorem is Theorem B.9 of the paper. Its hypotheses are D2, `EnumComplete L` (`Tammes15/Hyps/Computations.lean`, Definition 7.1: every rotation system with 12 to 15 vertices of a 3-connected graph with degrees 3 to 5, in the input class of plantri read on a drawing, `Tammes15.D2Regions.PlaneClass`, is isomorphic, possibly reversing the orientation, to a member of `L`); a family `N` of interval procedures (`Tammes15/PaperSteps/SearchDefs.lean`, Definition 6.3); `Impl N` (`Tammes15/Contractors/Defs.lean`, Definition B.1): on every box of the domain, each narrowing of `N` is the outcome of a run of the primitive steps defined in `Tammes15/Contractors/Prims.lean`, computed with some sound arithmetic, and each enclosure contains the triangle angle of the primitive steps, computed with some sound arithmetic; and `ProgTreesDom L N`: every case of `L` has, for every choice of hexagons, a choice in the same faces with accepted trees from boxes of the domain that contain the root boxes, on ranges of $`d`$ that cover $`[d_{lo}, d_{hi}]`$. D2 is given by the enumeration of plantri and the trees by the replayed certificates; `Impl N` follows from what Section 9.6 of the paper assumes of the program. `Tammes15.Contractors.killed_of_progTreesDom` gives D3 from the same hypotheses. The third theorem is Theorem B.8, from D2 and D3 (`Killed`, Definition 7.3).

The proof contains the structure theorem of the paper, Lemma 4.9 on the regions of a drawing, the inequality of its local optimality theorem, the rigidity bound D1 at the two optima (`Tammes15.Kappa.kappaHyp`, by an exact certificate), the attainment D4 (`Tammes15.Attained.attained`, by identities in the field of degree 20 and interval bounds), the numerical margins of Section 4.3, the bound $`d_{15} \le 56.6716^\circ`$ (`Tammes15.fejesToth_bound`, slightly weaker than that of Fejes Tóth), and the soundness of the contractors of the program for every sound arithmetic (Proposition B.2, `Tammes15.Contractors.Impl.onDom_sound`), each with no hypothesis; the non-isomorphism of the contact graphs of $`C_1`$ and $`C_3`$ is `Tammes15.Nonunique.frames_not_iso`, with no hypothesis. The package also proves the local optimality theorem with its equality case (`Tammes15.PaperSteps.local_optimality_frame`), item (3) of Proposition 3.1 (`Tammes15.PaperSteps.optima_four`) and the last sentence of Corollary 1.2 (`Tammes15.PaperSteps.frames_not_isometric`, `Tammes15.PaperSteps.frames_not_distance_preserving`), with no hypothesis. The data of the certificates of D1 and D4, the witnesses of the margins and the separations of the corollary are generated by the scripts of `code/lean-data/` and checked by the Lean kernel. `code/lean/check.sh` builds the package from source and checks the axioms, the sources and every constant (output `code/lean/check.out`). Comparator checks fifteen theorems against their statements in `Tammes15/Challenge.lean`, written again over copies of the 24 definition modules (`config.json`). The certifying run of the CI on the release commit runs it in nanoda alone, and its output is in the verification archive of the release.

What stays outside Lean is said in Section 9.6 of the paper: plantri, with the options of Computation 7.4, meets its description and the output filter keeps exactly the graphs of maximum degree at most 5, so that together they list every graph of the class up to isomorphism and reflection; and the program meets its model in the sense of Definition B.1(6): its arithmetic is sound, it numbers the variables of a case as the paper does, its narrowings are runs of the primitive steps of `Prims.lean` and its triangle angle is theirs, its first level reads every graph of the enumeration, rejects a graph only when, for every set of its hexagons, its narrowing sends a box of the domain that contains the root box to empty, and writes every other graph to the survivors, and its replay reports a graph verified on a range only when its procedures accept a tree on that range for every set of its hexagons. That the replay reports every survivor verified on ranges that cover the range of the program is an output of the replay and of the coverage join (Section 7.5).
